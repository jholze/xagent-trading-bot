## Release decision (Kay, framework 2026-11-01)

Expected effect: within 48 h all 7 `obs_*` collections have documents for each tenant; order counts per tenant/source unchanged against the tape trend.

Kill criterion (48 h): cycle-time median worse by more than 10 % vs the first 12 h after #742 deploy, or any trading-cycle exception from `observe_streams`, or a change in buy/sell counts that can be traced to macro observe. Any one -> revert.
