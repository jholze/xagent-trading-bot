## Rules (fixed values)

- `g_no_chase`: new long entry whose price is more than 10 % (RISK_OFF/CRASH), 20 % (RISK_ON) or 15 % (otherwise) above the minimum closed 1h low of the last 48 h (needs 36 bars) -> `would_skip_entry`.
- `h_oracle_early_exit`: lot pnl <= -10 % within its first 72 h and oracle RISK_OFF/CRASH -> `would_full_exit`.
- `e_age_limit`: lot older than 14 days and pnl <= -15 % -> `would_close_aged`. New rule; not harness scenario `e` or `f`.

## Release decision (framework 2026-11-01)

Area: observability. Not an exit, DCA, entry or sizing change. Merge only after the 48 h measurement of #738 on 3.11 (release rule 6). Kay merges.

Worst case: one extra Redis GET per order row (oracle read when the decision did not set it), one ccxt 1h fetch per new-entry candidate on the background thread, and extra Mongo inserts. The trading thread only enqueues; the queue drops on full.

Kill criterion (48 h): cycle-time median worse by more than 10 % vs the 12 h before deploy, or any trading-cycle exception or order rejection traceable to `shadow_rules` or the oracle stamp, or a change in buy/sell counts per tenant/source vs the tape trend. Any one -> revert. Faster switch: set `SHADOW_RULES=0` on xagent-test (stops the rules; the oracle stamp stays).

Revert plan: `git revert <squash SHA>` on staging. Only new `obs_shadow_rules` documents and the extra `ctx_oracle_state` values remain; both are inert and the collection expires by TTL (120 days). Record the squash SHA in /workspace/umbau/release_log.md.

48 h measurement (read-only): share of new orders with `ctx_oracle_state` not null per tenant/source; `obs_shadow_rules` counts by rule, action, oracle_state and tenant (dedupe lot rules on `lot_id` + `rule`); WARNING lines containing `shadow_rules`; cycle duration log lines.
