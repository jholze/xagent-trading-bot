# Evidence: DCA grace and the stop-resolver gap

Money-path change (DCA partial stop and full stop). The owner decides. This file does not add `owner-approved`.

Grace delays only the partial. The default window is 12 hours (`_DEFAULT_GRACE_HOURS`). The full stop still fires inside that window, from `average_entry`, on the cycle and the WebSocket path. After the window, open DCA rounds no longer keep the partial paused. `config.json` and the #719 resolvers are unchanged: strategy and risk still disagree, and the tests lock that table.

Staging's new safety pins disagreed with this spec. `test_partial_stop_paused_during_dca` asserted a pause for `{dca_rounds: 1, last_dca_at: None}` and got False. `test_partial_stop_stays_paused_while_rounds_remain_if_flag_off` asserted a pause for open rounds with the flag off and no timestamp, and got False. #722 names that change: a missing timestamp is outside grace, and open rounds do not keep the partial paused once the window has passed. The pins now expect a pause only while the timestamp is inside the 12-hour window. `docs/domain/prevention-rules.md` on this branch says the same.

## Release decision (Kay, framework 2026-11-01)

Area: DCA (partial-stop pause on lots with open DCA rounds). Rule 5 applies: the fault is visible in the open book and covered by the #722/#733 tests and mutants. Cohort is separate from #738: every lot in the table below has `dca_rounds` >= 1; the #738 lots (STRK, ULTIMA) have 0.

Open-book proof (`shadow_4w/open_lots_now.csv`, snapshot 2026-10-31 20:19 Berlin): unlocked DCA lots at or below −25 % whose newest DCA round is 26 or more days old, and with `hard_partial_stop_done` false (9 of them with `sold_percent` 0, RHEA 0.5):

| --- | --- | --- |

| guardrail: open lots at or below −20 % (all tenants) | 79 of 124 (63.7 %) | not higher |
| guardrail: capital in lots at or below −20 % | 184,294 USDT | lower |

Worst case: partial stop sells on DCA lots that would have recovered. No new buys, no DCA add, no change to size per trade, number of positions or stop distance.

Kill criterion (48 h, any one -> revert): a sell on a locked lot; a partial on a lot whose newest DCA round is younger than 12 h; any DCA add caused by this change; open lots at or below −20 % above 63.7 %; new exceptions in `strategies/dca.py` or `decision_engine`.

Revert plan: `git revert <squash SHA>` on staging. SHA in /workspace/umbau/release_log.md.

48 h measurement (read-only): partial and full stop fills on lots with `dca_rounds` >= 1 by tenant, `hard_partial_stop_done` on the 10 lots above, locked-lot sells (must be 0), share of lots at or below −20 %.
