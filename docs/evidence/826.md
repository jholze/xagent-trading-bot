## Route guard: fail-closed and remaining gaps

- **With `shorts.testnet_futures.strict_route: true`, every testnet SHORT/COVER is denied** at `mode_proof_missing`, and then at `testnet_env_aliases_live`. This holds until a production ModeProof producer and a separate testnet key env name exist (follow-up: to be filed by Kay). No synthetic ModeProof is built. Committed config has no `strict_route` and `testnet_futures.enabled=false`.
- The probe transport is built only after the transport-independent checks pass. A denial in pass 1 leaves the ccxt session as it is.
- One ccxt client serves testnet spot and swap. Its `NoRedirectSession` therefore also covers testnet spot orders and the `execution/recovery.py` startup reads.
- **Remaining gap:** those reads cannot follow a redirect, but `authorize_route` does not check them (follow-up: to be filed by Kay).
- The swap copies `trust_env` from the replaced session (default `exchange.requests_trust_env`, False in ccxt), along with `verify`, `proxies` and headers. No proxy or netrc from the environment is picked up by the swap.
- `NoRedirectSession` sets `follows_redirects = False` and `max_redirects = 0`, and forces `allow_redirects=False`. Test: a 302 comes back after one send. A plain session follows the redirect.

## Measurement plan (paper, staging)

Tagged lots exist only when `shorts.sandbox_expiry_v220.enabled` is true and an entry stamps `sandbox_path = v220_paper`. Neither is committed.

Proof on paper is the line written after a tagged cover was sent, when halt, stale, timeout or the schedule shaped the decision:

```
short_cover tagged <SYM> halt=<bool|None> stale=<bool> timeout=<bool> sched=<set|none> -> <source> (<rationale>) executed=<bool>
```

- Railway logs (read-only): search `short_cover tagged`. Expect zero lines while the flag is off. Zero lines prove that untagged lots kept their path.
- With the flag on and a tagged paper lot:
  - A cover after 20:00 Berlin shows `(frozen cutoff or calendar day) executed=True`.
  - A stale cycle quote shows `stale=True … (price stale…)`.
  - Stale WS ticks never fill; they show up only in the hub stat `stale_tagged_short_deferred`.
- Route guard: search `futures testnet route denied`. It appears only when `strict_route` is literally true.
