## Honest caveats

- **In-sample.** The traits of the 4 coins were seen before the thresholds were fixed, so the
  catch test is in-sample. The thresholds are round values from existing specs (#643 F1, #747 G15), and
  the #745 run counts the five variants as trials (N = 55). DSR 0.11 is far from GO; OBSERVE means
  shadow first, not proof.
- **Survivorship.** The candle universe holds only pairs listed today. At least 35 Gate delistings from
  Sep–Oct 2026 are missing, mostly small caps, and the market-cap proxy is not fully point in time. Both
  make thin and small coins look too good, so the real effect of E1 is probably understated, while any
  mcap conclusion is overstated.
- Two of the four coins were **profitable** (H +881 USDT realized, AVAIL +83). A criteria rule blocks
  them anyway, including the profitable H entries.
- The recall cost is real: early recall of the scanner drops mainly through G15, because early gainers are
  often already more than 15 % above the 48h low. That is why the intended config runs G15 in shadow.
- The harness entry filter only filters entries; DCA evidence comes from the traits table, not the harness.
- Missing features keep the buy (`on_missing: keep`, same as the harness). This deviates from #643 R3;
  `on_missing: block` is available per tenant.
